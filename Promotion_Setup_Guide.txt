# Promotion Setup Guide for iTravelCruise System

## Overview
This guide provides a comprehensive overview of the steps to create promotions in the iTravelCruise system. Promotions are essential for attracting customers and enhancing sales.

## Prerequisites
- Access to the iTravelCruise system.
- Administrator privileges to create promotions.
- Familiarity with the promotion strategies of your organization.

## General Settings
- Log into the iTravelCruise admin panel.
- Navigate to the Promotions section.
- Click on "Create New Promotion" button.
- Fill in the promotion name and description.

## Applicability Criteria
- Define the eligibility criteria for the promotion, such as:
  - Customer groups (new customers, returning customers).
  - Specific products or categories.
  - Time frame for the promotion.

## Benefits
- Increased customer engagement.
- Higher conversion rates.
- Enhanced brand loyalty.

## Promotion Controls
- Set discount types (percentage, flat amount).
- Define maximum discount limits.
- Choose whether the promotion can be combined with other offers.

## Testing Considerations
- Test the promotion before going live by:
  - Creating test orders.
  - Checking application of discounts.
- Ensure that all conditions are applying correctly.

## Troubleshooting
- Common issues include:
  - Promotion not appearing on the website.
  - Discounts not applying at checkout.
  - Conflicts with other ongoing promotions.

## Summary Checklist
- [ ] Define promotion objectives.
- [ ] Set eligibility criteria.
- [ ] Review and test the promotion.
- [ ] Launch promotion and monitor performance.

## Conclusion
Following this guide will help ensure that promotions are created effectively and meet the goals set by your organization.
